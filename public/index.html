Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel an order?

Find your order in the Manage, Track & Cancel Orders section and click Cancel Order. Orders that are Pending or Processing are cancelled immediately and the items return to stock. Orders already Shipped or Delivered cannot be cancelled.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. Create an account to see your full order history in one place.

Can I place bulk or custom wholesale orders?

Yes. Use the contact form below or reach our sales desk on WhatsApp for custom quotes on high-volume orders.
